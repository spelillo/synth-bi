Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy covers Synth BI at bi.synth-sql.com. Synth BI is part of Synth and is run by SPE ("we", "us"). You sign in with your Synth account, which is covered by Synth's privacy policy too.

What we collect

  • Your account: Synth BI uses your Synth account (email address and password, stored hashed by our sign-in provider), plus which version of the Terms and this policy you accepted and when.
  • Dashboards you save: only when you sign in and save: the tables' data, the tiles, layout, settings, confirmed relationships, a thumbnail image and the assistant conversation.
  • The time of each AI assistant request, to apply the daily usage limit.

Without an account (Lite Mode) we don't store anything about you. Synth BI doesn't use analytics or tracking of any kind.

How your files are processed

Your CSV, JSON and Excel files are loaded into a SQLite database inside your browser tab, using WebAssembly. Queries, charts and exports (Excel workbooks, Tableau data sources, dashboard images) are made on your device. Your data only leaves your device if you sign in and save a dashboard. If you open a saved Synth workspace in Synth BI, its files are read from your Synth account.

The AI assistant

When you use the assistant, we send our AI provider, Groq, your message, your tables' names, column names and types and row counts, your dashboard's tile titles, chart settings and queries, and recent messages in the conversation. If a drafted query fails, the database's error message (for example, "no such column") is sent so the assistant can fix it. Your row data isn't included: queries the assistant writes run in your browser and only you see the results. Anything you type yourself is sent, so if you paste data into a message, that text goes too.

Replies and drafted tiles are written by AI and are labeled that way in the app. They can be wrong.

We don't use your files, dashboards or conversations to train AI models, and we don't let anyone else do so. [check Groq's current API terms and data retention for this account, then state here whether Groq keeps or trains on prompts.]

Cookies and local storage

If you sign in, a sign-in cookie keeps you signed in across bi.synth-sql.com and synth-sql.com. Your browser's local storage keeps interface preferences, such as the assistant panel's width. None of this is used for tracking or advertising, and there's nothing to opt in or out of.

How long we keep data

  • Saved dashboards and their data: until you delete them or your account.
  • AI request times: 30 days.

Deleted data can stay in our providers' backups for a short time before it's overwritten.

Your choices and rights

From the Account panel (click your email at the top of the app) you can:

  • Download my data: a file with every dashboard you've saved, including its tables' data.
  • Delete account: permanently deletes your Synth account and its data in both Synth BI and Synth.

You can also delete single dashboards from the home page. To correct something, object to how we use your data, or ask what we hold, email synth.sql@gmail.com. We'll answer within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have further rights under local law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority. We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account, and Synth BI isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Saved dashboards are only readable by your account, connections use HTTPS, and passwords are stored hashed by our sign-in provider. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects your data we'll tell you as the law requires.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we'll update the date above. If a change is significant, we'll let account holders know by email or in the app before it takes effect.
